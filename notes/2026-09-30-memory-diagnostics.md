## Claim and measurement

## Decision

Keep operational history useful. Failure counts, queue depth, latency, growth, and enum health status do not become zero because a displayed claim is forgotten. The governed diagnostic reader projects typed operational fields and filters proposal display text against the current policy. It checks exact physical source paths. Unavailable authority produces an explicit unavailable result, rather than a healthy empty view.

The diagnostic reader has its own purpose. Reusing the turn-start reader would silently limit a seven-day diagnostic view to 500 rows. A 1,001-row control retains the full requested window. The response refuses a projected payload above three mebibytes, instead of truncating history or exceeding the four-mebibyte native connector buffer. Whole-file input reads remain unbounded.
